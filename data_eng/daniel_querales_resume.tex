\emph{A data exploration project of the COVID-19 dataset. Used SQL to explore the values of total cases, total deaths \& people fully vaccinated. The data was visualized with Tableau Public tools}.

Data cleaning operations. Extract insights about the best products sales, the best months for sales, products often sell together and most frequent hour for sales }.
